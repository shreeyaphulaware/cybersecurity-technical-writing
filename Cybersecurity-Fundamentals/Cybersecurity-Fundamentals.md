## Introduction


In today's digital world,almost everything depends on technology.But have you ever wondered how this information is protected?

This is where Cybersecurity comes into the picture.

1.What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity refers to the practice of protecting computer systems,networks,applications and data from unauthorized access or cyberattacks. It involves implementing technologies,security processes and policies to ensure that digital information remains secure.

Example: When you log into your banking application using your username,password and OTP multiple security mechanisms help protect your account from unauthorized access.

2.The CIA Triad

The CIA triad is one of the most important concept in cybersecurity. It represents three fundamental principles.

Confidentiality

Confidentiality ensures that the information is acccessible only to authorized individuals. Example:Using passwords and encryption to protect confidential files.

Integrity

Integrity ensures that information remains accurate and is not modified without authorizarion. Example:Using hashing algorithms such as SHA-256 to verify file integrity.

Availability

Availability ensures that systems,applications and information remain accessible when required. Example:Regular backups and protection against cyber attacks.

3.Common Types of Cybersecurity Threats

Understanding common cyber threats is essential for identifying and preventing attacks.

1.Phishing

Phishing is a social engineering attack in which attackers send fraudlent emails or messages to trick users into revealing sensitive information.

2.Malware

Malware is a malicious software designed to damage systems, to steal sensitive information or gain unauthorized access.

Examples include:

-Viruses

-Worms

-Trojans

-Spyware

-Ransomware

3.Ransomware

Ransomware is a type of malware that encrypts a victim's files and demands payment to restore access.

4.SQL Injection

SQL Injection is an attack in which malicious SQL statements are inserted into application inputs to manipulate database queries

4.Major Domains of Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity consists of multiple specialized domains.

-Network Security:Protecting computer networks from unauthorized access and attacks.

-Application Security:Identifying and fixing vulnerabilites in software applications.

-Cloud Security:Protecting cloud infrastructure,services and stored data.

-Information Security:Protecting the confidentiality,integrity and availability of information.

-Endpoint Security:Securing devices such as laptops,desktops and mobile phones.

5.Basic Cybersecurity Best Practices

Everyone can improve their digital security by following these practices:

1.Use strong and unique passwords.

2.Enable Multi-Factor Authentication.

3.Avoid clicking suspicious links and attachments.

4.Use trusted antivirus and endpoint protection software.

5.Backup important information regularly.

Conclusion

Cybersecurity is an essential part of today's digital environment.Understanding fundamental concepts such as CIA Triad,common cyber threats ,security domains and security tools provides a strong foundation for learning advanced cybersecurity topics.

Whether you are a student, beginner or a aspiring cybersecurity professional,building a strong understanding of these fundamentals is the first step towards developing practical security skills.

Keep learning,keep exploring and stay secure!
